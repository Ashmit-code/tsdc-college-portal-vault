About Our Institution

Three decades of excellence in education, research, and community service.

Thakur Shyamnarayan Degree College, established in 1993 under the aegis of Thakur Educational Trust, is one of Mumbai's most distinguished institutions of higher learning. Affiliated to the University of Mumbai, our college offers a wide array of undergraduate and postgraduate programs across Arts, Science, and Commerce streams.

Our campus is equipped with state-of-the-art laboratories, a digital library with over 50,000 volumes, modern auditoriums, and dedicated research centers. We take pride in our faculty — a team of accomplished academicians and industry professionals who mentor students to achieve their fullest potential.

With a strong emphasis on holistic development, our students consistently excel in academics, sports, and extracurricular activities, making TSDC a preferred choice for aspiring professionals.
